The CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog tracks CVEs that have been confirmed as actively exploited in the wild. Federal agencies are required to patch listed vulnerabilities by the stated deadlines. This dashboard pulls live data from the catalog and surfaces exploit lag, vendor exposure, and remediation status across all entries.
