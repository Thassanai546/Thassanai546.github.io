I made this page because Final Fantasy VII is, without question, one of the most important games ever made, not just to me personally, but to the medium as a whole. Released in 1997 on the original PlayStation, it proved that games could tell stories with genuine emotional weight, complex characters, and a world that felt worth caring about. For its time, it was staggering and beautiful.

On a personal level, this game fundamentally changed how I think about myself and how I communicate with others. Despite being heavily limited by 32-bit hardware, it achieved something profound. The pre-rendered backgrounds above are a perfect example—each one a hand-painted environment that forced a constrained system to feel vast and alive. In the same way, the characters' blocky models and text-based dialogue forced me to read between the lines, teaching me how to look past surface-level limitations to find deeper emotional truth and connect more authentically with the people around me. This page is a small nod to the team at Squaresoft who built something that clearly stuck with a lot of us for a very long time.
